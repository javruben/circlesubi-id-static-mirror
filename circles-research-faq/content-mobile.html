    Search the FAQs

    How is the value of Circles Points determined?

    1 Circles Point can be equivalent to 1 banana. However, the value of each individual Circles Point can be determined at the time of an exchange, or within a community.

    Can Circles Points be exchanged for or valued as a currency?

    No. Circles is not money or a currency. Circles Points cannot be exchanged for any currency. Circles Points are designed as a complementary means to facilitate the exchange of goods/services to support people in meeting their daily needs. Circles Points can only be used by Circles users, and transactions of Circles Points are based on the values of mutual support and mutual trust.

    How can I exchange for goods or services using Circles?

    Circles app users can exchange goods/services with Circles Points or a combination of Circles Points and cash. The value of goods/services is determined by the users involved in the exchange. For example, you can agree to give 10 Circles Points or a combination of 5 Circles Points with Rp5.000 for one bowl of porridge. You can also continue to negotiate with the user you are exchanging with to determine the value of the exchange until you have reached a mutual agreement.

    Can Circles users only exchange Circles Points with other Circles users?

    Circles users can only exchange Circles Points with other people who are using the Circles application.

    Why can I not exchange my Circles Point with someone?

    Before you can exchange Circles Points with someone you need to trust each other on the Circles App.

    I have registered as a user, but have not done any transactions What should I do?

    If you are a Circles App user but never use the App, neither you nor your community will benefit from Circles! So, make sure you use Circles Points as often as you can!

    Can I use my Circles Points outside of my village?

    Technically you can use your Circles Points to transact with any Circles user anywhere, however if they are far away from you it may make the exchange of goods or services difficult.

    If transacting parties cannot agree on the value of an exchange what happens?

    The exchange of goods or services for Circles Points only works when there is an agreement between the transacting parties on the terms of the exchange. You can continue to negotiate until you have reached a mutual agreement.

    Who determines the value of Circles Points?

    The value of Circles Points is determined through agreement between the people doing an exchange. The determined value is based on the principle of mutual support and trust.

    Can Circles support positive social impacts? How?

    Yes! Circles has many benefits for community welfare, including: Support locally produced products and services Economic participation for all (including vulnerable people) Help families & communities meet their daily needs Increase cooperative strategies in communities Support transparency & good governance Zero-cost social welfare support Increase market access for micro businesses Increase community-driven social security Support digital literacy Help micro & small entrepreneurs grow Empower women Demonstrate cost-effective UBI strategy for Indonesia Stimulate local economic growth

    Does Circles help community members exchange more goods with each other?

    Yes. Circles provide complementary means in the form of Circles Points to increase the exchange of goods/services within your local community. Everyday, the Circles app will provide 24 points to each users. This points are provided with the intention to serve as additional resource that can be spent to enhance their fulfillment of daily needs. Users can exchange Circles Points for goods/services with other users based on mutual agreement.

    Can Circles help fulfill family daily needs?

    Yes, Circles can help users fulfill their family's daily needs by exchanging Circles Points for goods / services with other users based on a mutual agreement.

    Can Circles help with my children's education?

    Circles can support users with the educational needs of their children by providing additional resource in the form of Circles Points. Users can exchange Circles Points for services, such as private tutor with other Circles users that provide that service.

    Can Circles help me learn new things?

    As the first technology innovation of a cashless implementation of Universal Basic Income (UBI) in the form of Points, Circles offers the opportunity for new experiences. By using the Circles app, users will be exposed to various interesting concept that combines technological advancement with local wisdom. For example, users will learn how blockchain technology can be used to support the spirit of mutual cooperation and sharing by generating Circles Points that can be used by users to support the growth of their local economy.

    I don't have enough Circles Points for the transaction I want to do. Can I use a combination of Circles Points & Rupiah?

    Yes, in addition to exchanging goods/services with Circles Points, users can also use a combination of Circles Points and cash to exchange goods or services with other users. However, it important to understand that the method used and the value of the exchange is based on mutual agreement between the users involved.

    Who can transact using Circles Points?

    All Circles app users can transact using Circles Points.

    How many Circles Points do you get each day?

    Each user will receive 24 Circles Points every day.
